Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 14, 2026

1. Local Browser-Based Media Processing

CutPulse Studio operates exclusively as a client-side web application. When you select video or audio files for editing, those files remain strictly inside your browser's local memory (RAM) and are processed using WebAssembly and Web Audio technologies. We do not upload, transmit, store, or view your media files on any external server.

2. Advertising & Third-Party Cookies (Google AdSense)

We use third-party advertising companies, including Google AdSense, to serve ads when you visit our website. Google uses cookies (such as the DoubleClick cookie) to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites on the internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

3. Log Files & Analytics

Like most standard web servers, our hosting infrastructure may collect non-personally identifiable technical metrics such as browser type, device category, referring pages, and anonymized aggregate traffic counts to maintain service stability and security.

4. Contact

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out via our Contact Page.
