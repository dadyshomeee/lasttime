Zero-Latency Local Rendering

Traditional cloud editors force you to upload gigabytes of raw footage and wait in render queues. CutPulse compiles WebAssembly video pipelines directly inside your browser for instant local processing.

Transient Audio Peak Detection

Our Web Audio analyzer filters sub-140Hz bass frequencies across kick and snare channels, mapping millisecond-accurate timestamps so every scene transition lands cleanly on the beat.

Smart 9:16 Subject Framing

Convert horizontal 16:9 widescreen clips into vertical Shorts and Reels effortlessly. Computer-vision landmark tracking keeps speakers and high-motion action locked in the center frame.

How Browser-Based Beat-Sync Editing Works

CutPulse combines three native browser technologies—MediaPipe Vision, Web Audio API, and client-side WebAssembly—to automate repetitive timeline cutting without cloud servers. When you load a video and select a rhythm profile, the engine extracts audio transient peaks, identifies high-motion visual frames, and aligns your cuts to the exact tempo of your soundtrack.

Are my videos or audio files ever uploaded to a server? +

Never. All video decoding, motion analysis, beat detection, and MP4 encoding happen strictly inside your device's local browser memory. Your source files never leave your computer or phone, guaranteeing complete privacy and zero upload wait times.

Which video and audio formats are supported? +

CutPulse supports standard web-compatible containers including MP4 (H.264/H.265), MOV, and WebM for video, alongside MP3, WAV, OGG, and AAC for custom audio tracks. Final exports are rendered in crisp 720×1280 vertical MP4 format ready for YouTube Shorts, TikTok, and Instagram Reels.

How do I get the cleanest beat-sync results? +

For fast-paced gaming, automotive, or sports montages, upload a raw clip longer than 10 seconds with clear movement and pair it with a high-contrast bass track (125–150 BPM). For talking-head podcasts or commentary clips, switch to the Clean Cut (Auto-Silence) mode with 9:16 Auto-Center framing.
